About Heart2Heart 💜

Heart2Heart connects kids (ages 6-17) who are going through tough times. Chat 1-on-1 with someone who truly understands.

How it works:

1 Pick a topic you want to talk about
2 Get matched with another kid (just 2 people per chat)
3 Chat and support each other

Safety Features:

✅ No personal info shared
✅ Messages are scanned for safety
✅ Report button for bad behavior
✅ Parent consent for under 13
✅ Crisis helpline info (988)
